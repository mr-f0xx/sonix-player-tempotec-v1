Design and behaviour

Background: #1a1b26; cards use #24283b and pressed states use #2d3554. Dark and Light remain available in Appearance.

Navigation: section tiles and browse controls open their own preview screens; back buttons and the top tabs remain usable throughout.

Music data: the recent rows are derived from the active queue in this prototype, not represented as persistent listening history. Add your own queue rows below to preview the layout; the firmware will read its real queue and library.

Icons: function-matched line glyphs from the repository, with the Flappy Bird tile drawn as a centered bird glyph rather than bitmap artwork.

On-device scope

Appearance keeps the existing theme, accent, dynamic tint, clock, boot-screen, text-size, font, and battery controls. Wireless preserves the Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, AirPlay, transfer, SonixLink, and DLNA destinations. Audiobooks keeps Library, Series, Authors, Continue, Finished, and Bookmarks. More retains DAC, optional Gearboy, Books, File Manager, and Flappy Bird.
